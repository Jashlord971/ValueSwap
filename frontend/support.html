Frequently Asked Questions

How do trades work?

Browse offers in the P2P Market, click "Trade" on any offer, and follow the steps to complete the exchange. The platform holds funds in escrow until both parties confirm.

How do I create an offer?

Go to My Offers and click "+ Add Offer". Choose whether you want to buy or sell crypto, select a payment method and currency, set your profit rate, and post.

What cryptocurrencies are supported?

CardSwap currently supports BTC, ETH, USDT, and USDC.

What happens if a trade goes wrong?

Open a dispute via the trade chat. Our moderation team will review the evidence and resolve within 24 hours.
